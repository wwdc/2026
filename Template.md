# Leo: An interactive journey through food desensitization

> **Leo** is an interactive App Playground created for the Apple Swift Student Challenge. It helps children on the autism spectrum explore new foods through gradual exposure, play, and emotional safety.

## 📖 About the Project
Food selectivity is a common challenge for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder, often leading to stress during meals and limited nutritional variety.

**Leo** was designed to transform this sensitive moment into a safe, playful, and progressive experience.

Instead of forcing direct interaction with food, the app introduces a **desensitization journey**. Users accompany Leo through small, meaningful steps — from observing food, to interacting with it, and eventually becoming comfortable with it.

By combining storytelling, interaction, and emotional feedback, the app promotes **confidence, autonomy, and positive associations with food**.

---

## Key Features
* 🧠 **Gradual Desensitization Journey:** Structured progression from visual recognition to interaction, respecting each child’s pace.
* 🍎 **Interactive Food Exploration:** Users engage with foods through playful interactions instead of pressure-based tasks.
* 🎮 **Gamified Learning:** Rewards and positive feedback reinforce small achievements and build motivation.
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Emotional-Centered Design:** Focus on reducing anxiety and creating a safe environment for experimentation.
* ♿ **Accessibility First:** VoiceOver, audio cues, high contrast, Dynamic Type, with support for Portuguese (PT-BR) and English (EN).
---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
The project was built entirely within the Apple ecosystem, following modern development practices:

* **Swift & SwiftUI:** Declarative UI, animations (`withAnimation`) and reactive state management.
* **Swift Concurrency:** Dialogue pacing and user feedback handled with `async/await` and `Task`.
* **AVFoundation:** Immersive sound effects and positive reinforcement audio.
* **UIAccessibility:** Inclusive design with screen reader support and real-time announcements.
* **Core Motion:** Detects tilt to move the avocado across the screen.
* **SpriteKit:** Handles the scene, animations, and movement of elements on screen.

  

---

## 🧪 Design and Research Process
This project was guided by both empathy and research:

1. **Problem Understanding:** Study of food selectivity behaviors in children with autism.
2. **UX/UI Design:** Prototyping in Figma focusing on reducing cognitive overload and creating a calming interface.
3. **User-Centered Iteration:** Continuous refinement based on usability testing and interaction feedback.
4. **Specialist Insight:** Consideration of therapeutic approaches like gradual exposure and positive reinforcement.

## 📱 How to Run the Project
For the best experience, the app was designed for iPad:

1. Open the `.swiftpm` file in **Xcode 26** (or later).
2. Select the **iPad Pro (11-inch)** simulator.
3. Run the project (`Cmd + R`) and start Leo’s journey!
